FAQs

Answers about video limits, copyright, subtitles, and refunds.

What video length works?

✕

Klipso AI handles videos up to three hours long. The AI scans the full file and extracts only the highest-retention moments for your clips.

Is it copyright safe?

✕

You keep full ownership of your content. Klipso AI only repurposes videos you upload or link from your own channel.

Which subtitle languages?

✕

Klipso AI supports 30+ languages for karaoke captions. The AI transcribes and styles subtitles in the language you choose.

What is the refund policy?

✕

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on Pro and Agency plans. If Klipso AI does not work for you, contact support for a full refund.

Can I cancel anytime?

✕

Yes. Cancel your subscription in one click from your account settings. You keep access until the end of the billing period.
